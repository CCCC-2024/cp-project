
Direct/Global separation
Create a static scene, and generate the Floodlit, Direct and Global images of that scene. Put them in the report and point out features that are in the direct image vs. the global, to explain that your result is correct. Example features:

Specularities in direct image
Shadows in direct image are dark
Interreflections in global image
